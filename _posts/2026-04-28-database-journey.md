Before university, the word "database" meant nothing to me. When Dr. Bilal Ahmad introduced Database Systems in our first semester at UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus, I was starting completely from zero.

## Why Do We Need Databases? 🤔

The first thing Dr. Bilal Ahmad made us understand was the "why" behind databases:

- Why not just store data in files?
- Why does structure matter?
- Why do relationships between data matter?

Data is everywhere. Every app you use, every website you visit, every system you interact with — there is a database behind it, quietly organizing and serving information.

## The Building Blocks 🧱

The fundamental concepts came first:

- **Tables** — organizing data into rows and columns
- **Primary Keys** — unique identifiers for each record
- **Foreign Keys** — connecting tables together
- **Relationships** — how tables relate to each other

## Dr. Bilal Ahmad's Teaching Style 👨‍🏫

Dr. Bilal Ahmad always taught with real world scenarios. He never just gave us abstract definitions — he showed us how databases work in actual systems, actual applications, actual problems that real engineers solve every day.

## Lab Sessions 💻

Lab sessions brought everything to life:

- Writing actual SQL queries
- Working with real data
- Creating tables and inserting records
- Building relationships between tables
- Seeing query results appear on screen in real time

## Final Reflection #

What this journey gave me was not just knowledge of databases — it gave me a new way of thinking about information. How to organize it. How to structure it. How to retrieve it efficiently.

My database journey is still just beginning. But every journey starts somewhere — and mine started in Dr. Bilal Ahmad's classroom, from absolute zero, one table at a time.
