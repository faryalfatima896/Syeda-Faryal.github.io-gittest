If someone had told me at the start of my first semester that I would be making Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and completing assignment after assignment — I would have said "that sounds easy." But nothing about first semester was easy.

## What Is AICT? 📖

AICT — Introduction to Computer and Information Technology — covers the basics of computers, MS Word, PowerPoint presentations, and fundamental computer skills. Sounds simple on paper. But when you are a fresh student, still adjusting to university life and hostel life — even the "simple" things feel heavy.

## The Assignments 📝

The assignments came one after another:

- **MS Word** — proper formatting, headings, tables, and layouts
- **PowerPoint** — design, content, and flow
- **Computer Basics** — understanding hardware, software, and systems

Each one had its own requirements, its own deadline, and its own challenges.

## The Struggle Was Real 😓

All of them were tough in their own way. Word felt frustrating when formatting would not cooperate. PowerPoint felt overwhelming when you had to make something that actually looked presentable. The difficulty was not just the work itself but the pressure of doing it all while managing everything else university life throws at you.

## The Relief of Submission 😮‍💨

But there is something deeply satisfying about finishing something that felt impossible. When I submitted that last assignment — the relief was indescribable. That feeling of "I did it" after days of stress and struggle is something no one can take away from you.

## What AICT Taught Me 🎓

- How to use computer tools properly
- How to present information clearly
- How to work under pressure and deadlines
- The importance of computer basics in everyday life

## Final Reflection #

Every assignment I completed made me a little more confident. A little more capable. And that confidence — built through struggle, late nights, and the relief of submission — is something I carry into every challenge that comes after this.
