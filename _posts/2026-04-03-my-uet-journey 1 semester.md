## How It All Began - The ECAT Struggle

Getting into UET Faisalabad was not easy. ECAT preparation was one of 
the most stressful periods of my life. Waking up early, studying late 
at night, solving endless MCQs, and constantly worrying about whether 
I would make it or not. The pressure was real. But I kept going, and 
alhamdulillah, it paid off.

## First Day at UET

Walking into UET for the first time was overwhelming. The campus was 
huge, the crowd was unfamiliar, and everything felt new and scary. I 
remember thinking — "Can I really do this?" But slowly, day by day, 
I started finding my place.

## Hostel Life - Living Away from Home

Living away from home for the first time is a completely different 
experience. No mom to wake you up, no home-cooked food, and no one 
to remind you about deadlines. You learn to be independent very 
quickly. It is tough, but it teaches you so much about life.

## 1st Semester - The Real Challenge Begins

1st semester hit hard. New environment, new people, and subjects that 
felt completely foreign. But the most challenging of all was 
**Programming Fundamentals (PF)**.

## Programming Fundamentals - The Subject That Tested Me

PF was honestly one of the most difficult subjects I had ever 
encountered. Seeing code for the very first time was so confusing. 
Understanding the logic, figuring out why something works and why 
it does not — it felt impossible at first.

I spent hours staring at my screen, trying to make sense of 
everything. Many times I felt like giving up. But with practice 
and patience, things slowly started clicking.

That moment when your code finally runs without errors — 
there is no feeling like it!

## 2nd Semester - Growing Stronger

Now in my 2nd semester, I look back at 1st semester and smile. 
The girl who could not understand basic programming logic is now 
writing programs confidently. Every semester brings new challenges, 
but I know now that I can handle them.

The journey is tough, but it is worth it.
