Every journey has a beginning. Mine started with a dream, a lot of stress, and an ECAT form.

## The ECAT Struggle

I still remember the days I spent preparing for ECAT — the anxiety, the late nights, the constant self-doubt. Coming from Lahore, the pressure was real. Everyone around me had opinions about what I should study, where I should go. But deep inside, I knew I wanted Computer Engineering.

ECAT wasn't easy. It tested not just my knowledge, but my patience and willpower. There were moments I wanted to give up. But I didn't.

## Getting Into UET Faisalabad

When I saw my name in the merit list for BS Computer Engineering at UET Faisalabad, I couldn't believe it. All those sleepless nights had finally paid off. It was one of the proudest moments of my life.

## Leaving Lahore

Leaving Lahore was harder than I expected. Lahore is not just a city — it's home. The food, the streets, the people, my family. Moving to Faisalabad for university meant stepping completely out of my comfort zone for the first time.

Hostel life was a culture shock. Suddenly I had to manage everything on my own — my time, my meals, my emotions. It was overwhelming and exciting at the same time.

## First Semester at UET

My first semester introduced me to the world of programming through Python. I had never written a single line of code before. Watching my first program run successfully was a feeling I can't describe.

The highlight of my first semester was my **Machine Learning project** — I built and trained an ML model as part of my finals. For someone who had just started coding, it felt like climbing a mountain. But I made it to the top.

## Being an Introvert in University

University is loud. People are everywhere — in classrooms, hostels, canteens. For an introvert like me, it took time to adjust. I don't make friends easily. I prefer small, meaningful conversations over big social gatherings.

But I've learned that being an introvert isn't a weakness. It means I observe more, think deeper, and when I finally speak — it matters.

## Where I Am Now

I'm currently in my 2nd semester, diving deep into databases, SQL, and WAMP Server. Every day I learn something new. Every day I grow a little more.

This blog is my way of sharing that growth — quietly, honestly, one post at a time.
