When Dr. Bilal Ahmad assigned us our first Machine Learning project, I did not fully understand what I was getting into. We had a mall dataset — a real dataset containing different categories of products with various attributes. Looking at it for the first time, I remember thinking — what exactly are we supposed to do with all of this?

## The Dataset 🗃️

Our dataset came from a mall and contained:

- Different product categories
- Product attributes and features
- Sales and customer data
- Various numerical and categorical values

Working with real data for the first time was eye-opening. Data is never clean. Data is never perfect.

## The Libraries We Used 🛠️

- **Pandas** — for loading and manipulating the dataset
- **Scikit-learn** — for building and training the ML model
- **Joblib** — for saving the trained model
- **Matplotlib** — for creating visualizations and graphs

Understanding what each library does, why we use it, and how it fits into the bigger picture was itself a major learning curve.

## Building the Model Step by Step 🔨

1. Loading the dataset with Pandas
2. Cleaning and preprocessing the data
3. Selecting features for the model
4. Splitting data into training and testing sets
5. Training the machine learning model
6. Evaluating model performance
7. Saving the model with Joblib

## The Graphs Were Exciting 📈

Using Matplotlib, we created visualizations of our data — bar charts, scatter plots, distributions. Seeing data transform into a graph that actually told a story was a completely new experience. Suddenly the numbers meant something. The patterns became visible.

## Dr. Bilal Ahmad's Guidance 👨‍🏫

Dr. Bilal Ahmad guided us through everything step by step. He is an AI, ML and Deep Learning expert who believes in learning through doing. He did not just give us the answer — he gave us the tools and the understanding we needed to figure it out ourselves.

## Final Reflection #

When it all came together — when the model trained successfully and the graphs rendered beautifully — the feeling was incredible. We had taken raw data, processed it, visualized it, and trained a machine learning model on it. As first semester students, that felt like something truly special.
